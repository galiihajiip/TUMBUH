## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, mission-critical workspace tailored for Indonesian micro, small, and medium enterprise (UMKM) operators. The emotional tone merges institutional reliability with swift, tactical momentum: authoritative, calm, lucid, and radically devoid of decorative clutter.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Corporate Modern with High-Clarity Industrial Utility**:
- **Pristine Canvas Structure:** Stark white analytical cards resting on subtle cool-tinted bone-neutral foundations (`#F8F9FA`) to maintain supreme visual comfort during prolonged inventory and revenue auditing sessions.
- **Intentional Telecommunication Heritage:** Telkomsel’s iconic crimson is deployed strictly as an alert, focus, and progression accelerator—never as ambient decoration. Red signifies systemic momentum (the "Growth Loop"), active diagnostics, and prioritized operational interventions.
- **Dignified Entrepreneurship:** UMKM operators manage real livelihoods; thus, the UI foregoes patronizing consumer gamification in favor of structured financial maturity tiers, crisp telemetry badges, and uncompromising data legibility.

## Colors

The palette enforces a rigorous hierarchy designed to isolate actionable business signals from background monitoring data.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary Red (`#EC1C24`):** Primary interaction trigger, loop bottlenecks, and urgent conversion milestones.
- **Deep Crimson (`#B5121B`):** Pressed/active state for primary actions and severe bottleneck alerts.
- **Soft Red Tint (`#FDECEC`):** Subdued contextual container fill for Telkomsel-powered interventions and critical metric dips.
- **Deep Charcoal Ink (`#1A1D23`):** Primary text and structural borders. Delivers near-absolute contrast against white cards without the harshness of pure black.
- **Slate Supporting Neutral (`#64748B`):** Secondary metadata, inactive states, and structural border framing.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface Canvas:** `#F8F9FA` — Crisp, cool foundation.
- **Surface Card:** `#FFFFFF` — High-focus foreground modules.
- **Surface Muted:** `#EEF2F6` — Dividers, input tracks, and inactive table headers.

### Tier Status Accents (Business Maturity Scales)
- **Bronze (`#A9713A` / Background `#FDF6F0`):** Foundational operations, initial formalization.
- **Silver (`#7F8A9B` / Background `#F1F4F9`):** Established cashflow, active digital channels.
- **Gold (`#C99A2E` / Background `#FCF8ED`):** Optimized multi-channel scale, automated operations.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and high-conviction numerical business metrics with **Inter** for all dense tabular views, status indicators, and form inputs.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Selected for its contemporary Indonesian tech vernacular—assertive geometric curves that maintain human approachability. Used exclusively for section titles, revenue rollups, and growth cycle stage markers.
- **Inter:** Chosen for extreme structural neutrality and tabular metric alignment. Financial data, comparative percentages, and diagnostic prompts must render with uncompromising clarity under adverse low-cost device screens and outdoor lighting conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **adaptive 12-column analytical grid** (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop) with a strict 4px baseline rhythm.

- **Mobile Breakpoint (&lt; 768px):** Compact vertical stacking. Canvas margin is `1rem` (`margin-sm`), gutter is `1rem` (`gutter-sm`). Horizontal scrolling is forbidden for primary growth cards; metrics collapse to 2x2 grid blocks.
- **Desktop Breakpoint (&ge; 1024px):** Fixed sidebar navigation (260px) alongside a fluid grid with a maximum content canvas width of 1440px. Gutters expand to `1.5rem` (`gutter`) and page margin is `2rem` (`margin`).
- **Rhythm Rules:** Within cards, component padding adheres strictly to `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) for parent cards. Gaps between metric micro-labels and numbers use `space-xs` (4px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally eliminates traditional heavy drop shadows, relying instead on **low-contrast architectural outlines and calibrated ambient depth** to keep the workspace lightweight and high-speed.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#F8F9FA` background.
- **Level 1 (Default Metric & Data Cards):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px hairline border in `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(26, 29, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Focus):** 1px border shift to `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0px 6px 16px -4px rgba(26, 29, 35, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Diagnostic Drawers & Growth Modals):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with an elevated ambient drop: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 29, 35, 0.12)`.
- **Bottleneck Spotlight Layer:** For growth cycle bottlenecks, elevate the card border with a solid 1px primary accent `#EC1C24` plus an ambient crimson wash (`box-shadow: 0px 4px 20px rgba(236, 28, 36, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces a dual geometric rule that separates structural content containers from interactive physical affordances:

- **12px Radii (`rounded-default` / `rounded-md`):** Assigned to nested data modules, analytical charts, form fields, and table wrappers.
- **20px Radii (`rounded-xl`):** Assigned to primary parent containers, overarching growth cycle cards, and featured tier elevation panels.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Exclusively reserved for interactive buttons, status badges, tier chips, and loop phase pills. This sharp distinction visually separates clickable actions from static structural frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Growth Button:** Full pill radius (`9999px`), background `#EC1C24`, text `#FFFFFF`, typography `label-md`. Hover: `#B5121B`. Active: `#8E0E15`. Height: 44px (touch-first baseline).
- **Secondary Action Button:** Full pill, background transparent, border 1.5px solid `#1A1D23`, text `#1A1D23`. Hover: background `#1A1D23`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary Utility:** Full pill, transparent, text `#64748B`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`, text `#1A1D23`.

### Data Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Connected Data Chip (Automated):** Background `#EEF4FF`, text `#2563EB`, border 1px solid `#DBEAFE`. Prefixed with a pulsating 6px green hardware signal dot.
- **Self-Reported Data Chip (Manual):** Background `#F8FAFC`, text `#64748B`, border 1px dashed `#CBD5E1`. Indicating manual user input awaiting verification.
- **Tier Badge (Bronze / Silver / Gold):** Pill container, uppercase `label-sm`. Gold uses `#FCF8ED` background, `#C99A2E` text, and a crisp 1px `#F3E8C4` border.

### Growth Loop & Diagnostic Indicators
- **Growth Loop Node:** Circular connector showing 4 loop phases (Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral). Active bottlenecks display a pulsing `#FDECEC` outer halo with a solid `#EC1C24` inner node.
- **Diagnostic Alert Card:** Border-left 4px solid `#EC1C24`, background `#FFFFFF`, interior containing headline in `title-md`, secondary text in `body-sm`, accompanied by a direct pill CTA.

### Form Inputs
- **Base State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, border-radius 12px, height 44px, text `body-md` (`#1A1D23`).
- **Focus State:** Border 1.5px solid `#1A1D23`, outline 3px solid `#FDECEC`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Control:** Radii 4px (Checkbox) or circle (Radio). Unchecked: 1.5px border `#94A3B8`. Checked: solid `#EC1C24` fill with pure white tick/dot.
